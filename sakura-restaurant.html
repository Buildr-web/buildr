Our Story

Born in Kyoto.
Brought to you.

"Food is not just nourishment — it is memory, culture, and connection."

Sakura was founded in 2010 by Chef Hiroshi Tanaka, who trained for over a decade in the traditional ryotei restaurants of Kyoto before bringing his craft to the West. Every dish on our menu carries a piece of that heritage.

We source our ingredients with the same care a Japanese craftsman brings to his tools. Our fish arrives daily from trusted suppliers, our rice is imported from Niigata Prefecture, and our miso is aged for twelve months in hand-crafted cedar barrels.
